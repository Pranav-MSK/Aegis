Workflows
=========

Alert Workflow
--------------

- Ticket Creation: A ticket is automatically created when an alert is
  triggered.
- Assignment: The ticket is assigned to an investigator and a
supervisor.
- Investigation: The investigator works on the alert and records notes
in the investigation_notes field.
- Reporting: After investigation, the final report is submitted.
- Review: The supervisor reviews the report and updates the ticket
status.
